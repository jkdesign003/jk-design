VIDEOS
------
The first 2 Marriage AI Videos are already connected to your Cloudinary links.

To add Normal AI Videos:
Open script.js and add objects inside normalVideos:

{title:"Normal AI Video — 01",url:"YOUR_CLOUDINARY_URL"},

You can add 3, 4, or more. Videos without URLs are not shown.
